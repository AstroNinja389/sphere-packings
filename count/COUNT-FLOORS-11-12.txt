FLOORS ON n - 1 PAIRS OF ONE CLASS: DIMENSIONS 11 AND 12                                   18 November 2026
=====================================================================================================================
WHAT IS CLAIMED (proof inputs are listed under "depends on")
  dimension 11: density of every lattice packing <= 0.090771844556272
  dimension 12: density of every lattice packing <= 0.065276234098413
Each number is the maximum of an audited density (valid for lattices denser than the complement) and the complement
(0.0905 in dimension 11, 0.065 in dimension 12); in both the audited density is the larger one.

THE THEOREMS (statements; proofs in the premise files, bound to the bundles by sha256)
  n11/TEN-PAIRS-PREMISE-n11-v1.txt: L of dimension 11, minimum 1, density > 181/2000. Any ten distinct pairs +-x_i of one
     class modulo 2L, each of squared length < 9/4, satisfy sum max(|x_i|^2, 2) > 20 + 3/4.
  n12/ELEVEN-PAIRS-PREMISE-n12-S3_4-v1.txt: L of dimension 12, minimum 1, density > 13/200. Any eleven such pairs satisfy
     sum max(|x_i|^2, 2) > 22 + 3/4.
  (n12/ELEVEN-PAIRS-PREMISE-n12-v1-EXCESS-1_2-for-reference.txt is the earlier theorem with excess 1/2, which the two
  texts refer to for the common steps. No bound of this repository uses it.)

WHAT A REPLAY FROM THIS REPOSITORY REPRODUCES
  python -B count/audit_count_bound.py count/n11/bundle.json OUT.json      (and n12)
     the class budget with the floor, in exact arithmetic, given the premise; agrees with audit.json in every field
     except the timing and the name and hash of the freshly written kernel audits.
  python -B count/check_det_n_v2.py 10 3/8     and     11 3/8             determinant lemma, last step (interval computation)
  python -B frame/check_odd_order_v1.py 10 3/8 3     and     11 3/8 3 5    odd-order lemma
  python -B joint/audit_joint_bound.py joint/n10/bundle.json OUT.json      the dimension-10 bound used in dimension 11
  (the dimension-11 bound used in dimension 12 is joint/n11)
WHAT IT DOES NOT REPRODUCE
  The table lemmas (I4), (I5) of the two premise files. Their tables (.npy and .json, about 100 MB) and the 111 cell
  files of dimension 12 are not here. Here are: the logs of the table runs, of the re-check by the second
  implementation and of the cell runs, the bookkeeping files (their hashes are quoted in the premise files), and the
  programs in count/lemma-programs. The programs are byte-identical copies; they were not arranged to run from this
  repository (they import modules of the project tree).

DEPENDS ON
  dimension 11: determinant lemma v2 in ten coordinates (DETERMINANT-LEMMA-GENERAL-N-v2.txt; v2 widens the range of v1,
     the change has had no separate review); joint/n10; odd-order lemma, order 3; table lemmas T2(10; 3/8; 53/1000,
     53/250) and U(10; 3/8; 53/250); the count auditor.
  dimension 12: determinant lemma v2 in eleven coordinates; joint/n11; odd-order lemma, orders 3 and 5; table lemmas
     T2(11; 3/8; 129/5000, 129/1250; -111/10) and T2(11; 3/8; 129/1250, 258/625; -3); the level limit SMALL3
     (SMALL3-BOUND-v1.txt), without which the second table lemma is not admissible; the count auditor.
  Thin places named in the premise files: 0.05291 against 0.053 and 3.939 against 4 (dimension 11); 0.02576 against
  0.0258 and 63/32 against 2 (dimension 12).

REVIEW
  An automated referee (not a human) re-derived each proof text and its numbers and found no error
  (n11/AUTOMATED-REFEREE-REPORT-review-T1.txt, n12/AUTOMATED-REFEREE-REPORT-review-S1.txt). It did not check the tables,
  the bookkeeping or the cell scheme. The table and re-check programs have one author. No human has reviewed any of this.

THE AUDITOR
  count/audit_count_bound.py is frame/audit_frame_bound.py with the floor on frame["count"] pairs instead of n and the
  complement density given explicitly in the bundle (it is part of the premise). Its header names the project file it
  was copied from by hash.

REVIEW OF THE LEMMAS BEHIND THE DIMENSION-15 FRAME FLOOR                                        summary v1, 2026-11-18
=====================================================================================================================
What this is: five automated reviewers (subagents of the session that produced the dimension-15 bound), each given
the documents and programs of one link but NOT the author's reasoning or conclusions, with the task of finding
errors. It is an independent re-derivation and attack by the same kind of system that wrote the proofs. It is NOT a
review by a human mathematician, and it does not make the result a published theorem.
The reviewers could not save their own report files; their scripts and logs are in the folders R1..R5 next to this
file, and their reports were delivered to the session and are condensed here (every ERROR/GAP/MINOR item is listed).

OUTCOME: no ERROR in any link; no uncovered case; no counterexample. Three gaps of exposition (now written in
DV-SCHEME-ADDENDUM-v1.txt) and a number of minor points.

R1  DETERMINANT LEMMA (../n16-fifteen-frame-theorem-20261112/DETERMINANT-LEMMA-v1.txt)
 Verdict: det Q >= 1 under the stated hypotheses is established, both cases; equality only at Q = I.
 Checked: every step by hand; the rank-two determinant identity in exact rational arithmetic (300 cases); checks D1-D4
 again in exact and ball arithmetic; own search over 150,416 admissible matrices plus 548 sign-pattern descents:
 smallest det at Delta = 3/8 is 1175/1024 = 1.14746 (claimed bound 1.1469); no violation of det Q >= Phi >= F, F_neg.
 MINOR: (1) positivity of the bracket at the largest gamma is used but not stated (true: 47/80 and 0.5795 at 3/8);
 (2) the range [-3/52, 1/4] of the logarithm inequality fits Delta <= 3/8 only (the inequality holds on (-1, 1/4]);
 (3) the lemma's own sanity sampler puts almost all one-negative samples outside Delta <= 3/8 (non-probative block);
 (4) two script assertions are weaker than the text (1.146 for 1.1469), one line of arithmetic is cryptic.

 MINOR: A1 Step 2 uses the sum bound where delta_i <= 1/8 suffices; A2 positive definiteness of Q not stated; A3 Step 4

 Not checked by R2: Theorem 1 itself (that it holds lattice by lattice), the cap formulas, the joint-cut assembly.

 GAP: the cell form of (L1) is not written in the text (it is correct; now in the addendum, section 2).

R5  NUMERICAL ATTACK ON LEMMA DV(21/52) AND ON THE INDEX BOUND
 Outcome: no counterexample. Largest psi(g) with N_Q(g) >= 2 and N_Q(2g) >= 2: -3.116 (needed: >= -1). Largest
 min(N_Q(g), N_Q(2g)) with psi >= -1: 1.7758 (needed: >= 2). Largest group with all non-zero classes valid: 16.
 Cyclic groups of order 17..24: best 1.41 to 1.48 (needed: 2).
 Method: for fixed g the best Q is found exactly by a linear programme with cutting planes and complete lattice
 enumeration; local search over g from about 770 starts in several families, both parities. Positive control: with the
 budget on sum delta raised, the same search finds violations from about 0.7 (0.404 is the lemma's budget).
 Limits: local search over g, floating point, non-cyclic groups of order above 16 not searched.
